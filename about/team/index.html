Meet the People Behind Family & Fellows Foundation

Family & Fellows Foundation was established in 2016 to honour the legacy of Muhammad Bashir Asi. Today our Board of Directors and Management Committee work hand in hand to deliver educational excellence, healthcare access, food security, disaster response, and social welfare across Pakistan.

How We Work

Our team follows an audited project life cycle that covers feasibility, board approvals, resource mobilisation, execution, and impact evaluation. Volunteers log hours through our portal, programmes are monitored with geo-tagged updates, and financials undergo monthly reviews so every rupee reaches the families we serve with dignity and discretion.
