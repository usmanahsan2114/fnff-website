About Us

Empowering Communities Together

Building a Better Future Through Impact

Family & Fellows Foundation is dedicated to creating lasting change through education, healthcare, food security, and disaster relief. We work directly with communities to address their most pressing needs and build sustainable solutions for a brighter future.

  • Empowering communities through education, healthcare, and sustainable development
  • Providing essential services to underserved populations across Pakistan
  • Building lasting partnerships for community-driven solutions
  • A world where every individual has access to quality education and healthcare
  • Communities that are resilient, self-sufficient, and thriving
  • Sustainable development that creates lasting positive change
  • Transparency and accountability in all our programs and operations
  • Evidence-based approaches that maximize impact and effectiveness
  • Continuous innovation in addressing community challenges

Frequently Asked Questions

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped our foundation

From a single vision in 2016 to a nationwide movement, every step along our road reflects community trust, responsive action, and lasting compassion.

2016
  • Start of the Family & Fellows Foundation under the leadership of educationist Muhammad Bashir Asi.
  • Learning Essentials Initiatives launched to equip underserved students.
2018
  • Food Division is established to address daily hunger.
  • Annual Ramadan Food Drive begins, supporting vulnerable families.
2020
  • Health Division is raised to champion medical access.
  • Annual Winter Wear Drive launches alongside the COVID-19 Assistance Drive.
2022
  • Disaster Management Division responds to devastating floods in Sindh and Balochistan.
2023
  • Bashir Asi Scholarship for Excellence opens doors for talented students.
  • Clean Water Storage Initiative expands through the Health Division.
2024
  • Formal registration as an NGO initiated with the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan.
  • The Knowledge Centre (Bashir Asi Campus) is initiated to deliver holistic education.
