Frequently Asked Questions

We assist anyone facing hardship, regardless of age, gender, religion, or background. Our team prioritizes cases according to urgency and the level of need so that the most vulnerable receive timely help.

Our core programmes focus on Educational Excellence, Health Care Initiatives, Food for All support, Disaster Management, and the Social Welfare portfolio. These include projects such as The Knowledge Centre, Bashir Asi Scholarship for Excellence, Learning Essentials Initiative, Clean Water Storage Initiative, Winter Wear Clothing Drive, Prosperity Pathway Project, and emergency relief operations across Pakistan.

You can reach our team 24/7 through WhatsApp at +92 326 8484360 or email info@familyandfellowsfoundation.org. We collect basic details, review each case, and connect applicants with the relevant programme such as medical assistance, ration support, or scholarships.

Donations are accepted through bank transfers, mailed cheques, or coordinated cash pickups. You can also message us via WhatsApp to request account details or to schedule a collection. Every donor receives an official receipt and can indicate a preferred programme for their contribution.

All funds are routed through official bank accounts, verified against statements, and recorded in audited ledgers. Monthly internal audits, annual activity reports, and financial statements provide donors with full visibility into how resources are allocated across projects.

Yes. Donors can direct their support toward a particular initiative such as scholarships, clean water, medical assistance, Ramadan rations, or the Prosperity Pathway loans. We honour programme preferences and share impact updates for designated gifts.

Volunteers support educational mentoring, medical camps, ration and winter drives, event logistics, and digital outreach. Reach out on WhatsApp any time to learn about current roles, onboarding, and how your skills can strengthen our programmes across Pakistan.

Each project follows a lifecycle that includes feasibility assessments, board approvals, procurement control, site inspections, geo-tagged progress updates, and post-completion evaluations. This structure ensures transparency, accountability, and measurable results for every initiative.

Assistance is provided discreetly, often after hours, without photography or publicity. Beneficiary data remains confidential and is handled only by authorised team members to ensure every individual feels respected and secure.

Follow our website, Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter channels for stories from the field. Donors also receive WhatsApp updates, newsletters, and detailed reports highlighting milestones such as new water tanks, scholarship placements, and disaster relief accomplishments.

Our registered office is located in Daulat Pur, Tehsil Pind Dadan Khan, District Jhelum, Pakistan. Please contact us via WhatsApp or email to schedule an appointment—we welcome supporters who wish to see our work first-hand.
