Our Founder

Muhammad Bashir Asi (1953 - 2023)

Mr. Muhammad Bashir Asi was an esteemed educationist and philanthropist whose vision and dedication transformed the lives of thousands of students. With a career spanning over 45 years, he served in various educational institutions in District Jhelum, including as Senior Headmaster at Raja Ghazanfar Ali Khan High School and Government High School Pindi Said Pur, and later as Principal at Government College for Elementary Teachers, Jhelum.
Throughout his life, Mr. Bashir Asi remained a beacon of hope for those seeking guidance, mentoring students and colleagues alike with wisdom, compassion and integrity. His unwavering belief in the power of education as a catalyst for change inspired him to establish the Family and Fellows Foundation in 2016, a reflection of his lifelong commitment to uplifting the underprivileged and empowering his community through learning.

Today, his enduring legacy continues to guide our mission. Through every initiative, we strive to uphold his values of empathy, service, and excellence, carrying forward his vision of building a brighter and more equitable future for all.
