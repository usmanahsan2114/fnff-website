Bashir Asi Scholarship for Excellence

The Bashir Asi Scholarship for Excellence supports meritorious students who lack the financial means to continue their education. Named in honor of our founder, Mr. Muhammad Bashir Asi, the scholarship removes financial barriers so talented students can focus on learning and achieving their goals.

At present, 15 students receive monthly support that covers tuition fees and related academic expenses—ensuring continuity in their studies and peace of mind for their families.

Summary

This merit-based programme is part of Family & Fellows Foundation’s commitment to Educational Excellence—uplifting bright minds with the resources and recognition they deserve.

  • Merit-based support for talented students with financial need
  • Named in honor of Founder Mr. Muhammad Bashir Asi
  • Monthly coverage of tuition and related expenses
  • Currently benefiting 15 students
  • Removes financial barriers to keep scholars focused on studies
  • Transparent, audited fund management
