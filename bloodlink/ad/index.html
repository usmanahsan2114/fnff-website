How Blood Link Works

A seamless process that connects those in need with life-saving donors in minutes.

Full Details
1
Download & Register
Install the free Blood Link Android app and create your donor or patient profile in under 2 minutes.
2
Send or Receive a Blood Request
Patients in emergency broadcast a request. Nearby verified donors receive instant notifications.
3
Connect & Save a Life
Donor and patient connect directly. No middlemen, no fees. Just life-saving action.
Earn Your Donor Badge
Every donation levels up your profile — Bronze to Blood Hero — recognizing your lifesaving commitment.
