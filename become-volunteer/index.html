Become a Volunteer

Serve with Family & Fellows Foundation

Since 2016 our volunteers have been at the heart of every initiative—supporting educational excellence, expanding healthcare access, addressing food insecurity, responding to disasters, and strengthening social welfare across Pakistan.

Guided by the legacy of Muhammad Bashir Asi, we collaborate with volunteers who believe in compassion with accountability. From mentoring students at The Knowledge Centre to distributing winter gift packages and managing emergency relief, every hour donated helps families regain dignity and stability.

  • Educational Excellence: Mentor scholarship recipients, facilitate Learning Essentials distributions, and design workshops for digital literacy.
  • Health Care Initiatives: Support clean water installations, coordinate medical assistance cases, and document impact for quarterly audits.
  • Food for All Programmes: Organise Ramadan ration drives, Eid assistance packages, and Prosperity Pathway follow-ups with beneficiary families.
  • Disaster & Social Welfare Response: Join rapid relief teams for drought and flood operations, or assist in marriage and medical assistance case management.
What you can expect
  • Structured onboarding: Orientation on our Code of Conduct, beneficiary privacy protocols, and project life cycle checkpoints.
  • Verified impact hours: Check-in/out through project leads and portal logging ensures accurate recognition in our tiered volunteer system.
  • Ongoing mentorship: Quarterly feedback loops, skills training, and opportunities to lead field assessments as you progress.
  • Transparent reporting: Access to audited impact summaries, enabling you to share data-backed stories with partners and donors.
  • 18,250+ lives impacted with volunteer support since inception.
  • 150+ community projects delivered in education, health care, food security, and relief.
  • 700+ donors and fellows collaborating with our volunteer network.
