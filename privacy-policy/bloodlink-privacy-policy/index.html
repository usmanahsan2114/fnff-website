Privacy Policy for Blood Link

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Last Updated: December 31, 2025

About This Policy

Blood Link is developed and operated by the Family and Fellows Foundation, a non-profit organization dedicated to community health initiatives in Pakistan. Blood Link is a coordination platform that connects blood donors with recipients through Blood Banks - we are not a medical service provider, blood bank, or healthcare facility. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Blood Link mobile application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Account Information: Full name, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
  • Identity Verification: CNIC number, CNIC front/back photos for profile verification - these documents are deleted after verification and are not permanently stored. In case Blood Donation request is for relative (only blood relatives authorized) then CNIC of recipient is requested and deleted after verification.
  • Health Information: Blood group, last donation date, eligibility status
  • Location Data: Current location for finding nearby blood donation requests (with your permission)
  • Contact Information: Address details for coordination purposes

Health-Related Data

  • Blood Donation History: Records of your donations, dates, locations, and units donated
  • Medical Eligibility: Information about your eligibility to donate blood - final eligibility must be confirmed by blood banks and recipients
  • Blood Requests: Details about blood donation requests you create or respond to

Technical Information

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version
  • Usage Data: App interactions, features used, session duration
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens for notifications

How We Use Your Information

Primary Purposes

  • Coordination Platform: Connect donors with compatible recipients based on blood type and location - we do not guarantee blood donations or act as a blood bank
  • Request Verification: Verify the authenticity of blood donation requests for platform integrity based on data provided by recipients
  • Communication: Send notifications about matching requests and app updates
  • Platform Safety: Maintain donation history and verify user identities for coordination purposes only

Health App Functionality

  • Blood Group Compatibility: Match donors with recipients based on medical compatibility. Final decisions to be done by Blood Banks and Recipients.
  • Donation Tracking: Monitor donation frequency to enforce 3-month cooldown periods
  • Location Services: Find nearby requests within your selected radius (5km, 10km, 20km)

Data Sharing and Disclosure

Within the App

  • Donors see: Recipient name, hospital, blood group needed, urgency level, general location of hospital where donation is required.
  • Recipients see: Donor response status, general donor information (not personal details)
  • Foundation Staff: Access to verification data through secure admin dashboard

Third-Party Services

  • Supabase: Database hosting and authentication (data processed in secure cloud infrastructure)
  • Firebase: Push notifications only (no personal data shared beyond device tokens)
  • No Data Sales: We never sell your personal or health information to third parties

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect user safety.

Data Security

Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: All data transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with authentication required
  • Database Security: Hosted on Supabase with enterprise-grade security
  • Regular Updates: Security patches applied promptly

Organizational Safeguards

  • Staff Training: Foundation staff trained on data protection
  • Limited Access: Only authorized personnel can access verification data
  • Audit Logs: All admin actions logged for accountability

Your Rights and Choices

Data Control

  • Access: Request copies of your personal data
  • Correction: Update incorrect information through app settings
  • Deletion: Request account deletion (subject to legal retention requirements)

Privacy Settings

  • Location: Enable/disable location services in app settings
  • Notifications: Control push notification preferences
  • Profile Visibility: No personal information except name or what explicitly shared for blood matching

Data Retention

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while account is active
  • Donation Records: Maintained for medical safety and verification purposes
  • Inactive Accounts: Data deleted after 2 years of inactivity
  • Legal Requirements: Some data retained longer if required by law

Account and Data Deletion

To request deletion of your Blood Link account and associated data, please email: privacy@familyandfellowsfoundation.org

Requests must be sent from the email address registered with the account. All requests are processed in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

Blood Link is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we discover we have collected information from a minor, we will delete it immediately.

International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in servers located outside Pakistan through our service providers (Supabase, Firebase). These providers maintain appropriate security standards and comply with international data protection requirements.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify users of material changes through the app or email. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Important Disclaimers

Platform Limitations:

  • Blood Link is a coordination platform only - we are not a medical service provider, blood bank, or healthcare facility
  • We do not guarantee blood donations or medical outcomes
  • Final blood donation eligibility must be confirmed by qualified blood banks and medical professionals
  • We are not responsible for off-platform communications between donors and recipients
  • All medical decisions and blood donation procedures occur outside our platform

User Responsibility:

  • Users must verify donation eligibility with appropriate medical facilities
  • Recipients and donors are responsible for all medical aspects of blood donation
  • Platform users should follow proper medical protocols and safety guidelines
